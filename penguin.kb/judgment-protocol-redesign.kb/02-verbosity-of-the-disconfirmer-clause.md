# Is a disconfirmer clause on every contestable judgment acceptable?

Posed 2026-09-19, unruled. A reader's-cost question for the user.

The clause is ~15 words per claim ("Wrong if the hook has an upstream
timeout, or if a stuck run can't hold the lock here"). It is content,
not ceremony -- it is what makes the claim falsifiable and lets the
reader see in advance whether an objection will land -- but it lands on
every claim in scope.

Scope line proposed to bound it: trade-offs, causal stories, and
recommendations against the user's stated preference. Not
implementation choices with no stake ("I'd use `sed` here").

## Reframed 2026-09-22

The question is no longer clause-per-claim cost inside a trigger body
(the trigger reaches <=22% of claims; see 01-). It is whether one
always-on line -- "a contestable judgment states when it goes stale"
-- earns its place in CLAUDE.md. See 08-.

## Bearing of the 2026-09-22 measurement

Adopting the clause is no longer "the fix" but one of two layers.
Its cost question stands as posed; its benefit is now bounded by
assert-leg recognition (~30% of contested claims today), which the
criterion itself may raise but cannot be assumed to.
