needs your word either way.

triggers, not hoisted up. The corpus test (01-, 06-) says a rule
delivered through this trigger reaches at most ~22% of the

Course, has its demand met in 37 of 38 concessions. Always-on posture
text is the only delivery in this corpus with a demonstrated
compliance rate (also `trigger-installation`'s 30/30 IMPERATIVE

would move a rule with 37/38 observed compliance into a delivery
