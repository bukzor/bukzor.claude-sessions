   in any order." This one depended on 01–03, and I wrote the

**No amendment to the law.** Two rules caught this and I shipped
anyway; a third rule catching what two already caught is the wrong
repair. The operative lesson is application, not text: noticing an
order-dependency is a stop signal, not a caveat to ship with.

The underlying disposition is unchanged and needs no ruling — when the
pass closes, it commits, by the exact paths on the parent entry.
